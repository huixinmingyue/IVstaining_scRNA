Core conclusion:
  In vivo oligo-CD45 labelling stratifies splenic B cells: subtype
  composition tracks the labelling gradient, the high versus low tails
  differ in niche occupancy, and highly labelled marginal-zone B cells
  differ transcriptionally from weakly labelled ones.
