Fig. 5 | In vivo oligo-conjugated anti-CD45 labelling stratifies splenic B cells

a, Experimental scheme. An anti-CD45 antibody carrying a DNA oligonucleotide
(PCR handles plus a 10x capture sequence) was administered in vivo; spleen was
harvested and processed by CITE-seq, so that each cell carries both a
transcriptome and a count of bound oligo-CD45 molecules.

b, UMAP of the splenic B-cell compartment after removal of cluster 4
(n = 5,740 cells), coloured by B-cell subtype and labelled directly on the
plot. Subtype sizes: follicular B (Gpr183+) 2,564; follicular B 1,871;
marginal zone B 721; follicular B [3] 547; immature/pre-B 37.

c, Oligo-CD45 copies per cell by subtype, shown on a log1p scale. Violins are
scaled to equal width; the inner boxes give the median and interquartile range
with 1.5x IQR whiskers, and points beyond the whiskers are plotted. Group
sizes are printed above each violin. P from a Kruskal-Wallis test across all
five subtypes.

d, Subtype composition along the labelling gradient. Cells were binned by
oligo-CD45 copies (x, bin median, log1p scale) and each panel gives the
percentage of cells in that bin belonging to one subtype. Point area is the
number of cells contributing to the bin. The grey line and band are an
ordinary least-squares fit with its 95% confidence band, shown to convey trend
direction only; the printed statistic is a Spearman rank correlation between
bin median and subtype share, computed across bins.

e, The same cluster-4-removed compartment split into low (<= 1 oligo-CD45
copies, n = 871) and high (>= 6 copies, n = 1,814) tails.

f, Subtype composition of the tails in e.

g, Differential expression within marginal zone B cells, comparing cells with
>= 6 oligo-CD45 copies against cells with <= 1 copy. Each point is a gene from
an export already restricted to |avg log2 fold change| >= 0.25 (n = 133), plus
Cd274 added from a Wilcoxon test because it is absent from that export
(log2 fold change 0.10, unadjusted P = 0.090). Red marks genes with
Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted P < 0.05 (n = 89). Dashed lines mark the
fold-change threshold and adjusted P = 0.05. Ringed genes are labelled.

Statistics. All tests are two-sided. P values in c and d are unadjusted;
adjusted P in g is Benjamini-Hochberg across genes as supplied in the export.
Cells derive from a single pooled preparation, so the panels describe
cell-to-cell variation within that preparation and carry no between-animal
error term. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
