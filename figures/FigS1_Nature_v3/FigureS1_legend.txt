Fig. S1 | Library quality, marker support and compartment-restricted programmes.

a, nCount RNA, nFeature RNA, mitochondrial percentage and a dissociation-stress
module (mean log-normalised Fos, Jun, Junb, Atf3, Egr1, Dusp1, Zfp36, Hspa1a,
Hspa1b, Btg2, Nfkbia and Ppp1r15a) in all 15,271 Fig. 1 cells. Violin, box
(median and IQR). IV-, extravascular, n = 7,519; IV+, intravascular, n = 7,752.
b, Dot plot of lineage markers across the display identities in Fig. 1d,e. Dot
area encodes the percentage of cells with detectable expression and colour
encodes mean expression z-scored across cell types and clipped to +/-2.5.
Cd8b1, not Cd8a, is the CD8 decision gene.
c, Wilcoxon volcano of IV+ versus IV- in B cells (n = 3,121 / 2,861). Italic
labels mark the 21 genes that are also significant between marginal-zone
(CITE C2) and follicular (CITE C0) B cells.
d,e, Same test in hard-gated true CD4 (n = 260 / 925) and true CD8
(n = 1,410 / 1,477). Labelled genes are the top 12 (d) or 10 (e) per direction
after removing ribosomal, mitochondrial, Gm* and shared technical genes
(Lars2, Cmss1, Gphn, Cdk8, Camk1d). Thresholds |log2FC| >= 0.5, adjusted
P < 0.001.
f, Plasma-cell expression of Ighm, Ighg1, Mki67, Sdc1 and Cxcr4 (n = 45 / 93).
Aicda is omitted because it is undetectable in both compartments.

Differential expression treats cells as the independent unit (one pooled sample
per compartment) and is descriptive. Source data are provided as a Source Data
file.
