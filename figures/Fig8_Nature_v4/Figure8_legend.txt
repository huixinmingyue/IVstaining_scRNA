Fig. 8 | Perivascular windows and vessel-contact scores in the EAE brain

a, Schematic of EAE brain sectioning, slice collection and Visium spatial transcriptomics. b, Laminin/DAPI sagittal overview. c, DAPI and Laminin/CD45. Dashed yellow boxes mark six windows: original L1–L3 and added L4–L6. d, Laminin/CD45 zooms. L1–L3 are the original three fields; L4–L6 are the added windows cropped from the high-resolution coronal plate. e, Visium H&E and cluster map with the six windows. Cluster identity is in the legend (0/1 neurons, 2 hippocampal neurons, 3 oligodendrocytes, 4 Purkinje cells, 5 granule cells, 6 meninges, 7 vascular-associated, 8 plasma cells). Cluster numbers are not printed on the maps. f, T-cell POS versus NEG prediction scores on the whole section (immune spots, n = 32,811). Boxplots compare all immune spots and, separately, spots inside the six windows, split as L1–L3 versus L4–L6. g, The same layout for microglia. h, Microglia POS versus NEG scores inside each window (L1–L3 left, L4–L6 right), with the same all-spot and window boxplots as g. Boxes show the median and interquartile range; whiskers extend to 1.5×IQR. Stars are two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests of POS versus NEG within each set. Window sets are tested separately because L4–L6 need not repeat the L1–L3 direction.

Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
