Fig. S4 | Anatomical landmarks and Fig. 3 T-subset programmes on the
Stereo-seq spleen section

a, Log-normalised expression of Cr2 (CD21), Fcer2a (CD23), Cd4, Jchain,
Marco and Adgre1 (F4/80) on the same section used in Fig. 4. Colour
limits are the 1st-99th percentile of each gene. Scale bar, 1 mm
(0.5 um per chip unit). n = 30,625 bins.

b, Mean log-normalised expression versus follicle-centred normalised
radius (0 = centroid of Louvain-5 follicle components; 1 = equivalent
cluster-5 edge). Ribbons show SEM. Bins with n < 30 are omitted.
The dashed line marks the follicle edge.

c, Seurat TransferData prediction scores for the five Fig. 3 T islands
(memory/activated CD4 T, naive CD4 T, naive CD8 T, memory-like CD8 T,
effector/cytotoxic CD8 T) on the same section. Titles and colours match
Fig. 3a,b. Plasma sequential scale; colour limits are the 1st-99th
percentile of each score.

d, Paired prediction scores in B/T-niche bins (Louvain 1+4+5+8;
n = 14,026). Left, memory/activated CD4 T versus naive CD4 T
(median paired Delta = +0.434, 95% CI +0.431 to +0.437). Right,
naive CD8 T versus naive CD4 T (median paired Delta = +0.125,
95% CI +0.123 to +0.128). Boxes show the median and interquartile
range; whiskers, 1.5x IQR; points, individual bins. Two-sided paired
Wilcoxon signed-rank test; P < 1e-16 for both contrasts. Names and
fill colours match Fig. 3a,b.

Spatial bins are the observation unit. One spleen section.
Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
