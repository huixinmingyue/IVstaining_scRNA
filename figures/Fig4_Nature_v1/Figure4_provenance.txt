Figure 4 | provenance
=====================

Content origin
  Rebuilt as a five-row main figure from two prior vector packages, copied
  unchanged into source_data/ and source_figures/. Nothing outside this
  folder was written to.

Panel map
  a  B-cell niche clusters 1, 5; Bcell.spcd452 in cluster 1 bins
  b  Cluster 5; Bcell.spcd45 in cluster 5 bins
  c  Peri-follicular clusters 4, 5, 8, 9; Plasma.spcd452 in cluster 9
  d  B/T follicular clusters 1, 4, 5, 8; CD4T.spcd45 in cluster 8
  e  Clusters 4, 8; CD4T.spcd45 in cluster 4

Reuse level
  Structural adaptation of the Fig. 4 v2 drawing code (shared section
  frame, sequential score ramp, paired Wilcoxon). Rows b and e keep the
  SI cluster subsets and signature columns; POS/NEG labels and unpaired
  tests were not carried over.

Reproduce
  Rscript scripts/make_figure4.R
  Rscript qa/preview.R

Image integrity
  No contrast adjustment, cropping or retouching. Colour-scale range is
  the 1st-99th percentile of the pooled low/high scores in that row;
  out-of-range bins take the end colour. Underlying values are unchanged
  and are in source_data_plotted/.
