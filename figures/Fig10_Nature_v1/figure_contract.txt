Figure 10 | figure contract (written before plotting)

Core conclusion
  Within the same MCAO brains, vascular-contact (MCAO_POS) microglia are
  shifted out of homeostatic cluster 0 into proliferative cluster 3 and
  inflammatory / phagocytic cluster 5 relative to parenchymal (MCAO_NEG)
  microglia; cluster 5 carries interferon and lipid-handling programmes;
  the transferred MCAO_POS fraction marks a restricted day-3 territory in
  which Lgals3 and Mki67 rise while P2ry12 falls; and module scores locate
  G2M in cluster 3 and IFN / SAMC / ICAM in cluster 5, with SAMC and ICAM
  higher in POS cells of cluster 5.

Evidence hierarchy

              i (module states)
  validation  d, e, g
  controls    a, b; P2ry12 and Prdx1 in f-h as internal negatives;
              DAM-core/DAM-like remaining low in cluster 5

Statistics needed

  h  Spearman + 2,000-resample bootstrap 95% CI; BH across 4 genes
