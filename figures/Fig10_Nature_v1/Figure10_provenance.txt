Changes relative to the previous MCAO Figure 9
  The H&E reference tile, the six-identity weight bars and the POS-fraction
  histogram are not drawn. The POS-dominant split at 0.5 remains the rule
  for g and is stated on the POS-fraction map and in the legend. Weight
  degeneracy (98.7% on the two cluster-3 identities) is kept in the legend
  and statistics file rather than as a dedicated panel.
  Panel i rebuilds the module heatmap from the stored cluster x contact
  means. Axis labels use NEG/POS to match the compact tiles; the legend
  maps them to MCAO_NEG/MCAO_POS. Tile numbers use the 5.2 pt floor.
  DAM core is labelled with the three genes actually scored (Clec7a absent).
  Panels g, h and i share one row.

Data completeness
  No cell, spot, gene or replicate was dropped for plotting. a-b, 63,654
  cells; c-d and i, 33,867 microglia; e, 4,971 genes; f-h, 2,573 spots.
