Figure 10 | statistics, sample sizes and integrity notes

GROUP DEFINITIONS
  MCAO_NEG  parenchymal immune cells, intravascular-stain negative (n1, n2)
  MCAO_POS  vascular-contact immune cells, intravascular-stain positive (p1, p2)
  All four samples are MCAO mice at 48 h after reperfusion, separated by
  intravascular CD45 staining and flow sorting. This is NOT naive versus stroke.

IMAGE INTEGRITY
  Spatial maps in f are vector spots. The stored section raster is not drawn.
  No brightness, contrast, gamma or channel edit. No scale bar (no calibrated
  pixel size). All spots are retained.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NORMALISATION LIMITATION FOR f-h
  Spatial values are raw counts; no per-spot total is stored, so they are not
  depth-normalised. Lgals3 and Mki67 rise in POS-dominant spots, which a
  global depth deficit cannot manufacture. P2ry12 and Prdx1 decreases remain
  unresolved between biology and capture.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
