Hard-gate audit (panel a)
=========================
Verdict: KEEP. The RNA counts > 0 gate is a conservative, internally
consistent definition of the mixed CD4_T object. It is applied unchanged
to panels b-c (UMAP by IV and composition) and is the x-axis of d-g and i.

Priority (mutually exclusive):
  doublet (cluster 2) > NKT (Klrb1c+) > DP (Cd4 and Cd8a/Cd8b1) >
  true CD4 (Cd4+ Cd8a- Cd8b1- Klrb1c-) >
  true CD8 (Cd8a+ or Cd8b1+, Cd4- Klrb1c-) > unassigned.

Why this is reasonable
- NKT before CD4/CD8 is required because NK1.1+ NKT cells can be CD4+ or DN.
- True CD4 requires both Cd8a and Cd8b1 to be undetected. That matches the
  Fig. 1 rule that Cd8b1 (not Cd8a alone) decides CD8, while still keeping
  Cd8a+ cells out of the CD4 set.
- Cluster 2 is a T-B doublet (Ms4a1 63%); it is not a T subset.
- Unassigned cells are 78% Cd3e+ with no lineage RNA: dropout, not a fifth
  lineage. They stay unassigned rather than being forced into CD4/CD8.

Limits (stated in the legend)
- Cd4 RNA is sparse, so true CD4 (n = 1,185 / 6,098) is a lower bound.
- Detection is RNA-only; protein was CD45-TotalSeqB and was not used.
- One pooled sample per IV compartment.

Panel h is not a cell classifier
- Rows are true-CD4 Intra DEGs; columns are ImmGen bulk spleen T/NK
  subsets (GSE109125). Colour is gene-wise z of log1p bulk counts.
- IV- up genes peak in naive CD4 / Treg; IV+ up genes peak in NKT / NK.
- Winning identity of a gene = the column of maximum z. Query cells are
  not assigned to ImmGen subsets.

Panel i tests those ImmGen identities on the gated cells
- Fig. 2d method: AddModuleScore of the top 30 ImmGen genes with
  |log2FC| >= 1 (Ig/ribo/mt/Gm removed).
- Naive CD4 / Naive CD8: CD8naive vs CD4naive axis.
- NKT: NKT vs naive CD4. NK: mature NK vs naive CD4.
- Scores are plotted by the panel-a classes, dodged by IV.
