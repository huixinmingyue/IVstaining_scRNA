Fig. 1 lineage display rules (v3)
=================================
Original CD4_T / CD8_T labels are replaced by the RNA-rule classes from
Figure_TNK_reannotation panel b. Non-T lineages keep their original labels.
No cell is dropped (n = 15,271).

T-lineage rule (applied only to original CD4_T + CD8_T; identical to panel b)
  Cd3 (Cd3e/d/g UMI > 0) defines T; Cd8b1 (not Cd8a) decides CD8 versus CD4;
  Klrb1c marks NKT-like; Ms4a1+Cd79a with Cd3 marks B-T doublets.

  B-T doublet        B_strong AND T_pos
  B contaminant      B_strong AND NOT T_pos
  NKT-like           T_pos AND Klrb1c+ AND NOT CD4+
  CD4/CD8 DP         T_pos AND CD4+ AND CD8b1+
  CD8 T              T_pos AND CD8b1+ AND NOT CD4+
  CD4 T              T_pos AND CD4+ AND NOT CD8b1+
  T other            remaining T_pos
  NK-like            Ncr1+ AND NOT T_pos
  unresolved         remainder

Display lumping on Fig. 1 / Fig. S1a (rare classes only)
  B contaminant (n = 12)  ->  B cell     (RNA identity is B)
  NK-like       (n =  2)  ->  NK         (RNA identity is NK)
  unresolved    (n =112)  ->  T other    (no CD4/CD8/NKT call)

Kept as distinct display classes
  CD4 T, CD8 T, NKT-like, CD4/CD8 DP, T other, B-T doublet,
  B cell, cDC2, NK, Plasma cell, Neutrophil

Original NK is not split (NK clean / contaminant is an audit result, not Fig. 1).
