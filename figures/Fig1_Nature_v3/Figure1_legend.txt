Fig. 1 | Intravascular antibody labelling separates intravascular from extravascular splenic
immune cells for single-cell transcriptomics

a, Immunofluorescence of a spleen section stained for B220 (blue), CD4 (red) and DCIR2 (white),
shown as single channels and as a merge. Scale bars, XX um [confirm calibrated length].

b, Immunofluorescence of a spleen section stained for F4/80 (red), laminin (PLM, magenta) and
CD45 (green), shown as single channels and as a merge. Scale bars, XX um [confirm calibrated
length].

c, Flow-cytometric separation of intravascular and extravascular leukocytes. Cells were labelled
in vivo with intravenously injected anti-CD45 (Intra CD45) and ex vivo with anti-CD45 (Extra
CD45); IV+ (POS) and IV- (NEG) fractions were sorted and processed for single-cell RNA
sequencing as depicted in the workflow schematic.

d, UMAP embedding of all 15,271 sorted cells, split by compartment and coloured by cell type.
Original CD4 T / CD8 T labels were replaced by an RNA rule: Cd3 (Cd3e/d/g) defines T lineage
and Cd8b1 (not Cd8a) decides CD8 versus CD4; Klrb1c marks NKT-like cells; Ms4a1+Cd79a with
Cd3 marks B-T doublets. n = 7,519 cells (IV-, extravascular) and n = 7,752 cells (IV+,
intravascular). Every recovered cell is plotted; no display subsampling was applied.

e, Cell-type composition of each compartment, expressed as the percentage of all cells recovered
in that compartment. Values are printed next to each bar. Lineages are ordered by mean abundance
across both compartments. The same identities and colours are used in d.

Canonical marker support for these identities is shown in Fig. S1a. Proportions in e derive from
one pooled sample per compartment, so no replicate-level statistical test was performed and no
error bars are shown. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
