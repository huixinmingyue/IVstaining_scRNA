Image integrity
- Raw coordinates, prediction scores and four-gene expression were extracted from the Visium object used for the source plate and stored as CSV.
- H&E raster is he_immune5.rds (600 x 600). One vertical flip aligns large imagerow with the top of the panel, the same flip as the source plate. Global fade only (0.9 toward white). No local contrast, crop of the section, or pseudo-colour of the H&E.
- No calibrated scale bar was present on the source plate. Spot positions are Visium image coordinates.
- Two spot columns are spatially detached from the tissue body: 21 spots near imagecol 83 (gap 56 px) and 39 spots near imagecol 530 (gap 38 px). They are plotted because they are in the object. Four of them rank inside a top-100 score list. They read as dotted lines beside the section. They were present on the source plate.
