Figure S8 v1 | provenance
=========================

Content origin
  New SI figure assembled from published vector sources:
  panels a-b and d from FigS6 Nature-vector (trajectory + heatmap);
  panel c from Fig6 Nature v3 panel f (violins + BH Wilcoxon table).
  Source tables, RDS objects, source figures and upstream scripts were
  copied into this folder. Nothing outside this folder was overwritten.

Panel map
  a    DDRTree by State
  b    DDRTree by CNS_IV / CNS_IVC
  c    cluster x group violins (P2ry12, Hexb, Il1b, Cxcl2, Cxcl10, Id2)
  d    50-gene pseudotime heatmap with programme sidebar

Reuse level
  Geometry follows the source ggplot recipes (DDRTree + MST; geom_violin
  with BH stars; geom_tile z-scored bins). Colours for POS/IV and NEG/IVC
  were unified to the trajectory palette. No cells were dropped.

Reproduce
  Rscript scripts/make_figureS8.R
  Rscript qa/preview.R

Image integrity
  Vector line art and ggplot tiles. Trajectory shows all 1,807 cells.
  Heatmap values are 8-bin means, z-scored. No microscopy.
