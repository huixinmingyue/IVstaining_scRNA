Core conclusion:
  In one EAE brain section the vascular-contact (EAE_POS / MgIV) and
  parenchymal (EAE_NEG / MgIVC) programmes partition in opposite directions.
  L1–L3 and L4–L6 are separate perivascular windows: L1–L3 is the original
  lesion set, and L4–L6 is shown at the same magnification and tested on
  its own. Cxcl10 detection remains enriched in vascular-contact microglia
  and decays with distance from large vessels.
