Fig. 9 | Vascular-contact and parenchymal programmes across L1-L3 and L4-L6 windows of one EAE brain section.

a, T-cell EAE_POS and EAE_NEG prediction scores on all 32,811 spots. Both maps share one colour scale (0 to 0.63). Dashed boxes mark L1-L3 and L4-L6. The violin is the within-spot EAE_POS minus EAE_NEG difference outside the windows, inside L1-L3 and inside L4-L6. The box is the median and interquartile range, and the white diamond is the mean. Labels are the two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test against zero, Benjamini-Hochberg across the three windows, and the matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation: outside r_rb -1.00, q < 1e-300; L1-L3 r_rb -1.00, q < 1e-300; L4-L6 r_rb -1.00, q < 1e-300.
b, Microglial EAE_POS and EAE_NEG scores for the same spots, on a shared scale (0 to 0.38), with the same within-spot difference, Benjamini-Hochberg within this panel: outside r_rb +0.50, q < 1e-300; L1-L3 r_rb +0.47, q = 1e-160; L4-L6 r_rb +0.74, q < 1e-300.
c, L1-L3 at higher magnification (8,376 spots in the frame, 4,656 inside L1-L3). Left, unmodified H&E. Right, the four scores in a and b.
d, L4-L6 in the same layout (17,504 spots in the frame, 8,982 inside L4-L6).
e, Within-spot EAE_POS minus EAE_NEG difference outside the windows (n = 19,173), inside L1-L3 (n = 4,656) and inside L4-L6 (n = 8,982). Violins show the full distribution, boxes the median and interquartile range, and white diamonds the mean. Labels give the two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test against zero, Benjamini-Hochberg across six tests, and the matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation. Two-sided rank-sum tests of each window set against outside tissue, Benjamini-Hochberg across four tests: T cell L1-L3 r_rb -0.24, L4-L6 r_rb +0.16; microglia L1-L3 r_rb -0.08, L4-L6 r_rb +0.24. The two window sets are not pooled.
f, Cxcl10 counts in EAE_POS (MgIV) and EAE_NEG (MgIVC) microglial spots. Grey marks every assigned spot. Dashed boxes are the six windows.
g, Percentage of microglial spots with at least one Cxcl10 count, for all microglial spots, L1-L3 and L4-L6. Bars are observed percentages and error bars Wilson 95% intervals. Facets use independent y axes because the L1-L3 interval is wide (EAE_NEG n = 3, of which 0 are Cxcl10-positive; EAE_POS n = 54). Two-sided Fisher exact tests, odds in EAE_POS relative to EAE_NEG: all spots, odds ratio 5.8 (95% CI 2.7-14.8), P = 5e-09; L1-L3, odds ratio Inf (95% CI 0.1-Inf), P = 1; L4-L6, odds ratio 1.9 (95% CI 0.8-5.4), P = 0.138.
h, Cxcl10-positive spots on the Vwf+ Cdh5+ vessel mask, the perivascular (<= 25 px) versus distal contrast, and the Cxcl10-positive fraction in 8 equal-count distance bins. Every spot, including L4-L6, enters this distance analysis. Fisher odds ratio 3.06 (95% CI 2.51-3.75), P = 6e-37. Spearman correlation of distance against Cxcl10 count: rho = -0.077, P = 7e-45. Distances are image pixels.
i, Microglial spots on the vessel mask, the distance-to-vessel map, and cell-to-vessel distance for MgIV versus MgIVC among all microglial spots, inside L1-L3 and inside L4-L6. Two-sided rank-sum tests, Benjamini-Hochberg across the three tests. Positive r_rb means MgIV is farther from the mask. L1-L3 contains 3 MgIVC spots. All spots r_rb -0.15, q = 6e-10; L1-L3 r_rb +0.11, q = 8e-01; L4-L6 r_rb -0.10, q = 4e-02.

Panels a-i are one EAE brain section, so n is spatial spots rather than biological replicates. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
